Binder discovery services

Bring us a target, an existing binder or two proteins you want to bring together. We’ll design for your assay, imaging probe or delivery format.

Turn larger binders into microbinders or peptides

We start with a larger protein scaffold and graft its binding motif into smaller formats. Then we screen hundreds of designs together and test which microbinders or peptides keep the interaction you need.

Target disordered regions

We design and screen binders against intrinsically disordered regions. These sequences can take on many conformations.

Bring two proteins together

An engineered binder can recruit one protein to another. We test whether the three form a complex.

Select with logic gates

We build screens around combinations of interactions. An AND gate enriches candidates that meet both criteria.

What do you need your binder to do?

Tell us where it should bind, how you’ll use it and what you need to measure. We’ll plan the screen and follow-up assays with you.
