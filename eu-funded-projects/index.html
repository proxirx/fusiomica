Startup Hub: Poland Booster

Through Startup Hub: Poland Booster, we are developing a platform that brings AI protein design together with laboratory production and testing. The project focuses on de novo binders, proteins designed on a computer for use in diagnostics, molecular imaging and the development of biological medicines.

The project connects

  • AI models for protein design
  • High-throughput protein production and purification
  • Experimental tests of binder selectivity and activity
